Thanks to Gabriel Hines '26, Dylan Herink '27, and Muhammad Saleh Haider '28 for your thoughtful work in making this site possible.

At Swarthmore College, special thanks to Amanda Licastro (Head, Digital Scholarship Strategies), David Obermayer (College Archivist/Records Manager, Swarthmore Friends Historical Library), and Roberto Vargas (Head, Research & Instructional Services). None of this work would be possible without your incredible expertise, support, and time.
