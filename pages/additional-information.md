In 1944 the Wartime Relocation Authority (WRA) invited George's parents to Philadelphia to run a hostel to help Japanese American families resettle as they were able to leave camps. The Inouye family took in more than 1,000 Japanese Americans during their time running the hostel on Chestnut Street. You can learn more about the Inouye Family hostel in, "A Story of Welcome: This Philadelphia hostel provided shelter for Japanese Americans escaping internment." by Heather Rigney Shumaker '91 (Summer 2026, Swarthmore College Bulletin).
